# Acme Cloud — Billing & Refund Policy

## Plans

- **Starter**: $29/month, 2 projects, community support.
- **Growth**: $99/month, 20 projects, Pro support.
- **Annual Growth**: $990/year (equivalent to 2 months free versus monthly Growth).
- **Enterprise**: custom pricing, annual contract.

## Refunds

Monthly plans may be canceled at any time. Unused time on monthly plans is **not refundable**.

Annual plans include a **14-day money-back guarantee** from the purchase date.
After 14 days, annual plan refunds are prorated only if Acme initiates a service discontinuation.

Charge disputes must be filed within 60 days of the billing date by emailing billing@acmecloud.example.

## Invoices

Invoices are emailed within 24 hours of a successful charge and are also available in the Billing console.
Sales tax may apply depending on customer location.
